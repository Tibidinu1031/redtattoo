# Real RED TATTOO photo research

Fetched and visually inspected on 2026-09-25.

27 original images are saved in this folder. Recommended finished-tattoo portfolio selection: photo numbers **3, 4, 6 through 21** (18 distinct finished works). Files are named `red-tattoo-cristi-nitu-N.jpeg`.

Photos 22 through 27 document Cristi Nitu tattooing and the studio. Photos 1 and 2 are artist portraits. Photo 5 is the original circular studio logo. These should not be counted or described as additional finished tattoo works.

All files were obtained from the public salon advertorial “Cel mai nou salon de tatuaje din Sibiu – Povești pe piele la Red Tattoo by Cristi Nițu”, Ora de Sibiu, 26 October 2020, and the attachment metadata for that article. They were not fetched directly from Instagram or Facebook. Many tattoo photos visibly include the matching RED TATTOO studio branding.

Source page: https://www.oradesibiu.ro/2020/10/26/povesti-pe-piele-la-red-tattoo-by-cristi-nitu-cel-mai-nou-salon-de-tatuaje-din-sibiu/

Public attachment metadata: https://www.oradesibiu.ro/wp-json/wp/v2/media?search=red-tattoo-cristi-nitu&per_page=100

`source-manifest.json` records each local filename, original accessible CDN URL, source page, attachment page, dimensions, visual subject, category and SHA-256 hash. `contact-sheet.jpg` and `contact-sheet-additional.jpg` are visual review sheets, not portfolio assets.

Limitations: official Instagram returned a fetch error; official Facebook returned a temporary-block/login page. Exact-name image search mostly returned unrelated artists, which were excluded. The resulting works are documented in the salon’s 2020 feature, so do not describe them as recent or as fetched from the current social accounts. No generated concepts or unrelated artwork were used. Source/photographer licensing is not specified in the source metadata.
